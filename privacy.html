Privacy in Quizkveld

Last updated: 2. november 2026 · Applies to the Quizkveld iPhone app and this website. The Norwegian version applies if the two differ.

In short: Quizkveld has no sign-in, no ads, no tracking and no analytics. Player names, scores and settings are stored on your phone, and we do not receive them. Purchases are handled by Apple. We only get information about you if you write to us yourself.

What the app stores on your phone

  • Player names or nicknames and the chosen age group, to show whose turn it is and pick suitable questions.
  • The current round, scores, used questions and result history (at most 100 rounds).
  • Your language, sound, voice and speed choices.
  • Question packs you have got, so you can play offline. They come with the app, and the app does not fetch them from any server.

We do not ask for a date of birth, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos or microphone. The app sends none of this to us or anyone else, and we cannot see it. If you back up your phone to your own iCloud or computer, this information may be included.

Legal basis: What is stored on the phone is needed for the game to work as you ask. We have no access to it, so we do not ask for consent, and there is nothing to withdraw.

Purchases and offer codes

Purchases and offer codes go through Apple and the App Store. Apple processes payment and your Apple ID under Apple's privacy policy. The app only learns whether a purchase is valid so the pack can be unlocked. We do not receive your name, email or payment details.

Apple gives us combined statistics on downloads and sales. If you have chosen to share analytics with developers on your iPhone, we may also get crash reports from Apple without your name. You control this in the iPhone settings under Privacy & Security.

Read-aloud and source links

The Ida voice is a computer voice made in advance with Amazon Polly and included in the app. Reading aloud sends no text anywhere. If the phone's built-in voice is used, that happens on the phone. Source links after an answer open in Safari, and the site you visit has its own policies.

If you write to us

If you email us, we get your email address and what you write. We only use it to answer you. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in answering messages (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). We delete the email when the matter is closed, and no later than 12 months after the last contact. The email is kept with Google (Gmail) under Google's privacy policy.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses to deliver and secure the site; see the GitHub privacy statement. Legal basis: legitimate interest in running the site safely (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). We do not use the logs ourselves. The site uses no cookies or tracking.

Transfers to the USA

Google and GitHub may process information in the USA. They state that they use the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the EU standard contractual clauses. The app shares no information with others.

Children and families

The app collects no information about children. The child choice only picks questions, and it stays on the phone. Nicknames are welcome. Children should talk to an adult before writing to us. In-app purchases need an Apple ID, and parents can manage purchases with Screen Time and Ask to Buy.

Deletion

Tap «Slett spilldata» (Delete game data) at the bottom of the app's start page. Names, scores, history and settings are deleted. Packs you have got stay, as they hold no information about you. If you delete the app, everything is removed from the phone. Purchases belong to your Apple ID and can be brought back with «Gjenopprett kjøp» (Restore purchases). To have an email to us deleted, write to the address above.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection. They apply to what we hold about you, which in practice is only email you have sent us. Write to us, and we answer within one month. You can also complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority, Datatilsynet.

Changes

If the app changes what it stores, we update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect.
