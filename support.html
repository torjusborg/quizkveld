Support

Quizkveld is offered by Torjus Borg, Helge Væringsaasens veg 18, 2408 Elverum. The app is in Norwegian, so the button names are given in Norwegian here.

Can't find the answer? Email torjusbok@gmail.com. Please mention your iPhone model and iOS version.

How do we play?

Choose who is playing (solo, head-to-head, teams or Beat the clock), pick question packs and tap "Start". The question and answers are read aloud. Tap the answer you think is right.

Can children and adults play together?

Yes. Choose "Child" for the youngest players and they get age-appropriate questions while adults get theirs. With children on the team, Beat the clock is played without a countdown.

How does read-aloud work?

Ida is a computer voice made with Amazon Polly. She reads questions and answers in Norwegian. You can turn read-aloud and sound effects on or off during a round and change the speed in the game and sound settings. No sound? Check the volume and tap the speaker button.

Can I play offline?

Yes. The core content and any packs you have got work without a connection.

How do I get my purchases back on a new iPhone?

Open «Utforsk quizpakker» (Explore quiz packs) and tap «Gjenopprett kjøp» (Restore purchases). Use the same Apple ID as when you bought them. The packs become ready again by themselves.

How do I use an offer code?

Got a link: tap it on your iPhone and confirm. Got a code: open the App Store, tap your profile picture at the top and choose "Redeem Gift Card or Code". Then open Quizkveld; the packs unlock by themselves. If needed, tap «Gjenopprett kjøp» (Restore purchases).

A purchase was cancelled or charged

You are not charged until Apple confirms the purchase. For payment or refund questions, go to reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete game data?

Tap «Slett spilldata» (Delete game data) at the bottom of the app. Names, scores, history and settings on the phone are deleted. Packs you have got stay until you delete the app. Purchases stay with your Apple ID.
